Chapter 01 · The Boss

One man. One fire.

File Nº 001
Asansol · 2025

Biswarup Adhikary

Everyone calls him "Biswa Daa"

He pays the bills, picks the starting XI, yells from the touchline, and still laces up when the team needs him. Biswa Daa didn't inherit a club — he founded one, out of nothing but stubbornness and a group of teenagers who had nowhere else to play. Fifteen years in local football. Twenty-plus kids mentored. Two RPL finals. Zero excuses.

If you don't bleed for the badge, don't wear it.

Chapter 02 · Manifesto

Five reasons you'll hate playing us.

Print · Vol. I
Paste on your wall
01

We run for 90 minutes.

Not 70. Not 80. The whole ninety. Then stoppage time. Ask the last five teams.

02

We don't have stars.

Every player tracks back. Every player defends. Every player can score on a good day.

03

We finish games angry.

Half our goals come after the 75th minute. Keepers hate playing us. Good.

04

Our bench is loud.

Touchline noise is a tactic. The subs never shut up. The coaches never sit down.

05

We know our ground.

Chitra Radhanagar. Uneven bounce. Sharp wind. Our home, our rules. Tourists get cooked.
